What I do

I apply custom plasters and paints to homes and commercial spaces around Charleston. We start with a conversation and a sample to make sure we're on the same page and go from there. I can adjust the finish according to your preferences. I want to make sure everyone involved is happy with the finished product

No. 1

Venetian plaster

Lime plaster troweled on in thin layers and burnished to your specifications. For walls, range hoods, fireplaces and bathrooms. Cloudy tonal movement, cool to the touch, and no two walls are ever alike.

No. 2

Wood graining

Hand-painted grain that reproduces the figure, pattern and open pore of real timber. Match a wood already in the room, or put a wood like finish on a surface that was never wood at all.

No. 3

Marbling

Faux marble and onyx painted entirely by hand. The look of rare stone for walls, columns and fireplace surrounds, at a fraction of the weight and cost. Can be used to match existing marble as well.

No. 4

Decorative painting

Custom painted finishes beyond flat color — layered glazes, tonal washes and specialty effects. Murals and painted subjects are an option too.

No. 5

Gilding

Gold and metal leaf applied to surfaces and architectural details. Gilding can bring light into a space or highlight a detail beautifully.

How I run a project

Four steps, no surprises. You'll always know what's happening next and what it will cost before my work begins.

Step 1Conversation & site visit

Tell me about your project, then I'll come look at the space, the surfaces and the light, and we'll talk through finishes together.

Step 2Estimate & samples

I'll give you a written estimate with the scope spelled out, plus a sample board of your finish to approve before you commit.

Step 3Prep & application

I prep the site and apply your custom finish.

Step 4Walkthrough

We'll walk the finished work together, I'll touch up anything that needs it, and I won't call it done until it looks right to both of us.
